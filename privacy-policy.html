Nichetool Studio is an independently operated digital products business (operated by sanericci) that designs and sells small, niche software tools and templates through Gumroad. This page explains what information this website collects, and how our Pinterest account and Pinterest API integration work.

1. This website

This site (nichetool-studio-site) is a static page that lists our products and links out to their listing pages on Gumroad. It does not set cookies, does not run any analytics or tracking scripts, and does not collect or store any personal information from visitors.

Clicking through to a product takes you to Gumroad, a third-party platform that handles checkout, payment, and account data under its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing Gumroad's Privacy Policy before making a purchase there.

2. Pinterest integration

We operate a Pinterest Business account for Nichetool Studio and use the Pinterest API to automatically publish Pins (an image, a title, a description, and a link to a product page) to boards that we own on our own account, on a scheduled basis.

This integration does not access, collect, read, or store any personal data belonging to other Pinterest users. It does not read other accounts' boards, pins, profile information, or analytics — its only function is publishing content to our own account.

Access tokens used for this integration are stored as encrypted secrets in our private automation system (GitHub Actions Secrets) and are never shared with any third party.
